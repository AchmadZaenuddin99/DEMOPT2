Careers

Karier

Open roles for people who care about systems, texture and the player’s time.

Open flagshipView atlas

Nacre / field notes

Build a world that rewards attention

The studio's fictional role briefs focus on how the work feels in the player's hands. They are not open vacancies.

Systems designer

Prototype a relationship between weather, routes and player knowledge, then explain what the player can reliably infer.

Environment artist

Make a place legible through material, wear and silhouette before adding signs or dialogue.

Narrative designer

Write conversations that remember choices without reducing every relationship to a reward transaction.

Patient worlds, deliberate systems ↗

Morrowglass Works is a fictional independent studio. Its practice is expressed through the choices a player can observe.
